Note&Me Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Note&Me is a music note-reading app for children and adults. We built it so that it does not collect any personal information.

What the app stores

Student names (a first name or nickname), the chosen animal avatar, practice settings, and practice history are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting a student in the app, or deleting the app, removes this information.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose “Say It” or “Play It”, the app uses the microphone during a practice run to recognise the note you say or play. Sound is analysed live on the device by Apple’s on-device frameworks. It is never recorded, saved, or transmitted. You can turn microphone access off at any time in the Settings app; tapping answers keeps working.

Purchases

The optional Full Version is bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment; we don’t receive your payment details or any personal information.

No accounts, tracking, ads, or analytics

The app has no login, does not use advertising, does not track you across apps or websites, and contains no third-party analytics or SDKs.

Children

Because no personal information is collected or shared, the app is suitable for children. Parents and teachers can delete a student’s data at any time from the “Who’s practicing?” screen.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
