Note&Me Support

Note&Me helps children (and grown-ups) learn to read music notes: treble, bass and grand staff, with tap, spoken or played answers, progress history and flash cards.

Common questions

How do I restore my Full Version purchase?
Open a student, tap the settings row (the one with the sliders icon), choose “Unlock the Full Version”, then “Restore Purchases”. Family Sharing is supported.

“Say It” or “Play It” doesn’t hear me.
Check that Note&Me is allowed to use the microphone (and, for Say It, speech recognition) in the Settings app → Note&Me. Speak or play clearly, one note at a time, in a quiet room. The first time you use Say It, the device may need a moment to download its speech model. You can always tap the answer buttons instead.

How do I delete a student’s data?
Everything is stored only on your device. On the “Who’s practicing?” screen, touch and hold a student to edit or delete them. Deleting the app removes all data.

Does the app collect any data?
No. See the Privacy Policy.
